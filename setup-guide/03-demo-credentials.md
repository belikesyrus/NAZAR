# Demo Login Credentials

After running `python seed.py`, these accounts are available:

| Role | Email | Password | Access Level |
|------|-------|----------|-------------|
| Department Official | admin@dosje.gov.in | Admin@123 | Full access |
| Inspection Officer | officer@dosje.gov.in | Officer@123 | Inspect, submit reports |
| Project Incharge | incharge@project.in | Incharge@123 | Project data |
| NGO Staff | staff@ngo.org | Staff@123 | Attendance, feedback |
| District Authority | district@gov.in | District@123 | District view |
| Beneficiary | beneficiary@example.com | Beneficiary@123 | Own data only |

## Demo Data Included

- 6 sample projects (mix of project, institute, NGO types)
- 12 CCTV cameras (2 per project)
- 6 inspections with different statuses
- 30 days of attendance records
- 5 alerts (various severities)
- 5 notifications
- 3 complaints/feedback
- 5 beneficiaries per project (first 3 projects)
